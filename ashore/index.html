    The rules in this model
    • 30 merfolk start in the Deep Sea with four ribbons: Voice, Hands, Sight, Walking.
    • Borders open only at low tide, one border per tide. The Shallows cost Voice. The Shore costs two more: you need three left, and you keep one.
    • Without Walking you move only while someone who can walk is next to you. Without Sight you drift. Without Hands you can’t give.
    • A giver with Hands and Sight walks to anyone within 8 m missing a ribbon she has, and gives it.
    • Land Dwellers face their dinner. Now and then one turns and notices the closest merfolk within 12 m. Ten seconds of attention and she is understood.
    • Nobody goes back. At dawn, everyone not understood becomes sea foam.
